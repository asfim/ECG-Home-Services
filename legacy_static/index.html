About the Service

ECG Home Service in Dhaka — why test at home?

An ECG (electrocardiogram) is a quick, painless test that records your heart's electrical activity. It helps doctors detect irregular heartbeat, heart attack signs, and other heart conditions. With our ECG home service, you no longer need to travel to a hospital or wait in long queues — we bring the same 12-lead ECG machine to your home.

Who should book a home ECG test?

  • Elderly or bedridden patients who can't travel easily
  • Heart patients who need a routine ECG for their doctor
  • Anyone with chest pain, palpitations or breathlessness
  • People with diabetes or high blood pressure
  • Pre-surgery, pre-employment or visa medical ECG

What's included in our ECG at home?

Every ECG home service visit includes a trained technician, sterile disposable electrodes, a printed ECG strip on the spot, and a digital report on WhatsApp. Choose the cardiologist review package for a written interpretation within 30 minutes. Our home ECG service runs 24/7 across Mohammadpur and nearby areas of Dhaka.
